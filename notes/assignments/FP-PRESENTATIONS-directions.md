Short presentations of projects during final period.

This presentation is an opportunity to show off your work at a high level to the rest of the class. This presentation is less formal than the write-up, but should still clearly describe the project’s motivation, methodology, and results.

Total points: 20 (7.3% of final grade)

You must include the following information (at least one slide each):

- Description of project (3 points)
- Methodology and evaluation (3 points)
- Results (3 points)
- Demonstration or examples from the model output (3 points)
- Conclusion (3 points)
- Any spiritual insights working this project has given you or how it could be used or extended to contribute to building the kingdom of God (1 point)

You will also be graded on the overall quality of the presentation (4 points):

- Presentation was well-timed, no more than 6-7 minutes total, including 2 minutes for questions, and discussion
- Presenter was well-prepared, spoke clearly, demonstrated mastery of the project topic, and answered questions well
