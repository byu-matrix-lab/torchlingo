# Final Project Proposal

*Transcribed from the Learning Suite assignment description on 2026-09-29. Learning Suite due date: Wed Nov 11, 10:00. Points: 10. Roadmap v6 due date: Wed Nov 11. Status: text carries last year's dates (Nov 10 and 12, Nov 6 review). Rubric tables reformatted; otherwise verbatim.*

Create a 5-minute presentation including 1-2 minutes for questions (no more than 5 minutes total!) of what you propose to do for your final project. It should include exactly one slide for each of the following 5 points, with sufficient detail for understanding what you will be doing:

1. a description of the topic/hypothesis you will investigate and/or component(s) you will create
2. the background documents/papers/other content you will study and why they are relevant (at least 3 references other than the ones we’ve covered in class)
3. the code you intend to write and/or the data/corpora you will use and how you will use them
4. the type and amount of evaluation you will perform. NOTE: For text output, you must include a human evaluation by at least 3 people across 100 sentences and an automatic evaluation using BLEU and/or a more recent metric, such as COMET. For speech, visual, or some other kind of output, you must clear your evaluation method with the instructor.
5. the outcome you expect to obtain and how this work could contribute to building the kingdom of God
